Meravs is a new watch label out of {{ city }}. The first collection is short on purpose — a few dials, chosen carefully, priced without the theatre.

The letters

Cut from Roman capitals

The name is set in Marcellus, a typeface drawn from Roman inscriptional capitals. That is why the dial carries Roman numerals: the wordmark and the hours come from the same source.

The colours

Taken from the first two watches

Navy by day and burgundy by night, with gold throughout. The navy and gold come from a blue sunburst dial and a gold case, the two watches the brand starts with.

The price

One price, printed plainly

No struck-through MRP, no permanent sale, no discount that was never a discount.
